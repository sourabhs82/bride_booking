Meet the Artist

Capturing Forever in a Single Frame

◆

Hello, I'm Isabelle — a bridal photographer with over a decade of experience documenting the most intimate, joyful, and fleeting moments of your wedding day. My style blends timeless film-inspired tones with a gentle, editorial eye that puts you at ease the moment we meet.

Every couple has a unique story. I believe the best photographs are not staged — they are found in a glance across the aisle, a whispered word, a tear that refuses to stay hidden. I am simply there to witness it and make it last forever.

Based in New York, available worldwide. Let's create something extraordinary together.

Isabelle Laurent
